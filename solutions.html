Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about pricing and our quotation process.

How long does it take to get a quote? +

Once we receive your requirements and land details, our engineering team typically provides a preliminary estimate within 24 to 48 hours. For highly customized projects, it may take slightly longer.

Is the initial consultation free? +

Yes, the initial consultation and quotation are completely free of charge. We believe in providing transparent pricing so you can make an informed decision.

What factors affect the total cost of a steel building? +

The primary cost drivers include the total floor area, the number of stories, material grades (finishing quality), foundation requirements based on your soil type, and location logistics.
