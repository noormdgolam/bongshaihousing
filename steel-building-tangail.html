Who We Are

Bangladesh's Most Trusted Housing Company

BONGSHAI HOUSING is a Bangladesh-based Real Estate and Housing Company. The Company undertakes projects on the basis of Engineering-Procurement-Construction & Commissioning (EPC&C), with the experience and resources to execute complex projects on time with international standards of quality, safety, and reliability.

The company always gears up to excel stakeholders' expectations with its modern technology and sophisticated, experienced professional workforce — responsible for the entire process from design and materials supply to construction and test operation.

  • ✔️ High strength, inflexibility and high load bearing capacity
