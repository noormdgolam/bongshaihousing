Cyclone-Ready Construction for Khulna

As a coastal port city facing regular cyclone exposure and salt-laden air, Khulna demands construction that goes beyond standard brick-and-mortar. Our steel-frame buildings are engineered for high wind-load resistance and built with corrosion-resistant materials suited to Khulna's maritime climate — ideal for residential, warehousing, and light-industrial projects across the district.
