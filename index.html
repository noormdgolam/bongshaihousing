Who We Are

Bangladesh's Most Trusted Housing Company

BONGSHAI HOUSING is a premier Real Estate and Housing Company serving all over Bangladesh — anywhere, anyplace. While we have major hubs in Dhaka, Chattogram, Gazipur, Cumilla, and North Bengal, we undertake Engineering-Procurement-Construction & Commissioning (EPC&C) projects nationwide. We have the experience and resources to execute complex pre-engineered steel buildings (PEB) and prefab housing on time with international standards of quality, safety, and reliability.

From affordable cottage projects in Bogra to luxury container resorts in Probachol — and every district in between — we gear up to excel stakeholders' expectations with our modern technology and sophisticated, experienced professional workforce, responsible for the entire process from design and materials supply to construction and test operation.
