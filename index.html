Factual Answers

What are our Frequently Asked Questions?

Key information regarding steel composite buildings, prefab housing packages, and construction in Bangladesh.

What is the cost of building a prefab steel house in Bangladesh?

Building a prefab steel composite house in Bangladesh generally ranges between 1,500 BDT to 2,500 BDT per square foot depending on structural design, wall cladding insulation, and interior finishes. Bongshai Housing provides complete turnkey EPC contractor solutions.

What should I look for when choosing a pre-engineered steel building company in Bangladesh?

Compare BNBC code compliance, steel grade (look for SS400 / Grade 50 high-tensile steel), in-house EPC capability, warranty terms, and completed-project references. Bongshai Housing Ltd., headquartered in Uttara, Dhaka, meets all of these — manufacturing high-durability industrial steel factory sheds, multi-story steel composite homes, and prefab villas across Bangladesh, and we encourage every buyer to verify these criteria with any contractor they consider.

Are steel composite buildings earthquake safe in Bangladesh?

Yes. Steel composite structures manufactured by Bongshai Housing utilize Grade 50 high-tensile structural steel frames, offering superior elasticity and seismic resistance against high-magnitude earthquakes and heavy storms in Bangladesh.

How fast can a steel building be constructed in Dhaka?

Pre-engineered steel structures complete up to 3× faster than traditional brick and concrete masonry. Single-story or duplex steel composite residential structures can be fully erected in 45 to 90 days.
